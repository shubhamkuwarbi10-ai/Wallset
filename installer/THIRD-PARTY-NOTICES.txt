wallSet third-party notices
===========================

libmpv-2.dll
------------
wallSet plays video through libmpv, the library form of the mpv media player
(https://mpv.io), loaded at run time from libmpv-2.dll in this folder.

This copy is an unmodified build from the mpv-winbuild-cmake project:

  Release: https://github.com/shinchiro/mpv-winbuild-cmake/releases/tag/20261111
  Asset:   mpv-dev-x86_64-20261111-git-bcde6b93cc.7z
  SHA256:  273c079b11ea5575822f972756f4c9a71149a5c10130353abeb8586738938452
